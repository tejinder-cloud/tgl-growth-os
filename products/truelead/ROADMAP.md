# TrueLead Roadmap
**Status:** Parked until TrueVisible ships (Architect Decision — 30 Oct 2026)

TrueLead provides lead scoring, caller workflow accountability, and conversion attribution. Core development is intentionally parked while the company focuses on shipping TrueVisible (AEO citation tracker and trial engine).

---

## Phased Versions

### Version 1 — TGL Clients Only (Private Pilot)
- **Target Audience:** Existing The Growth Lab retainer clients.
- **Scope:** 
  - Lead intake via Meta Lead Ads and direct CSV upload.
  - Mobile-first lead card for internal counsellors with two-tap outcome logging.
  - Basic daily CAPI feedback loop to Meta.
  - Internal dashboard for time-to-call, outcome rates, and weekly client reporting.
- **Pricing:** Included within existing agency retainers (₹0 standalone).

---

### Version 2 — Marketing Agencies (White-Label)
- **Target Audience:** Performance marketing agencies handling high-volume lead generation.
- **Scope:**
  - Multi-tenant agency dashboard with sub-accounts per client brand.
  - Agency-branded reporting and white-label client views.
  - Consolidated agency billing and multi-seat caller access.
  - CAPI event attribution mapped to agency Meta Business Manager.
- **Pricing:** TBD (Separate agency tier; per-client brand monthly fee).

---

### Version 3 — Coaching Institutes (Direct Self-Serve)
- **Target Audience:** Independent coaching institutes and test-prep academies (2+ branches, 150+ monthly enquiries).
- **Scope:**
  - Self-serve onboarding and Meta ad account connection.
  - Multi-branch hierarchy and counsellor shift tracking.
  - Automated WhatsApp alerts to leads and counsellors.
  - Admissions attribution analytics and counsellor performance scorecards.
- **Pricing:** TBD (Separate software subscription; monthly SaaS fee per branch).

---

### Version 4 — Larger B2C Prospects (Enterprise)
- **Target Audience:** Multi-city educational enterprises, healthcare networks, and high-ticket B2C services.
- **Scope:**
  - Custom CRM integrations (Salesforce, LeadSquared, Zoho).
  - Advanced AI lead scoring model (trained on ≥2,000 logged outcomes).
  - SLA enforcement routing and multi-tier escalation triggers.
  - Custom data retention, enterprise SSO, and dedicated compliance exports.
- **Pricing:** TBD (Separate enterprise contract; annual billing with SLA guarantees).
