Who we are

This website is run by The Growth Lab, India. For anything about your personal information, write to sales@thegrowthlab.co.in.

What we collect

  • When you ask for the free AI visibility audit: your business name, website, industry, city or market, your name, your email address and, if you give it, your WhatsApp number.
  • When you send an AI Automation enquiry: your name, business name, email address, the job you chose, anything you write in the message box and, if you give it, your WhatsApp number.

[If analytics is switched on: we also use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to see, in aggregate, how the site is used. What you type into forms is hidden from these tools.]

We do not ask for, and do not want, sensitive personal information such as health details, financial details or government identity numbers.

Why we use it

  • To prepare and send the report or reply you asked for.
  • To email you about that request and, if you gave a WhatsApp number and ticked the box, to message you there.
  • To understand which pages and campaigns are useful.

We rely on the consent you give when you tick the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time.

How the audit uses your details

To run an audit we ask AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude) questions that a buyer of a business like yours might ask. Those questions use your business name, industry and city or market, which are already public. We do not send your name, email address or phone number to the assistants.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the services that help us do the work:

  • our customer and messaging system, which stores your contact details and sends our emails and WhatsApp messages;
  • our hosting and database providers, which store the request and the report;
  • [if switched on] Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, for aggregate usage.

Some of these services store data outside India.

We keep your request and our correspondence for as long as we are in conversation with you or working for you, and for [period to be confirmed] afterwards, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or the law requires us to keep it longer.
