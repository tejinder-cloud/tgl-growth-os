    Questions

    Questions, answered.

    What is an AI agent? Is it a chatbot?

    A chatbot answers when someone types to it. An agent has a job: it starts on its own, follows a routine, uses your tools, and reports what it did. Some of its work is conversation; much of it is not.

    Do you bring us leads?

    No. We do not run ads or generate leads. The agents handle the enquiries you already get, so fewer of them go unanswered.

    Do you only automate lead follow-up?

    It is where we start most businesses, because every unanswered enquiry is a cost you can see. We take on other routine work case by case, and we will say plainly if a job is not a good fit for an agent yet.

    What does it cost?

    There is no price list, because the work depends on the job and on the tools you use. After a 20-minute call we give you a fixed quote and a date in writing.

    Which tools can the agents work with?

    The ones you already use for enquiries, messages, your customer list and your calendar. We confirm what can be connected on the call, before we quote.

    Will this replace my team?

    No. The agent takes the routine work, at any hour, and your team gets the leads that are ready and the decisions that need a person.

    What happens when an agent gets something wrong?

    Each agent has a person who checks its work, and at the start nothing reaches a customer without approval. When an agent is unsure it stops and hands over, and it says so in its daily report.

    Do you promise more sales?

    No. We promise that the job on the job card gets done, every day, and that you can see it. What that does for your sales depends on your business.

    Have you done this for others?

    We do not name clients on this site. What you can check today is our own company, which runs on agents built the same way.
