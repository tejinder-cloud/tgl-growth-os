    Questions

    Questions, answered.

    What is AEO, and is it the same as SEO?

    Related, not the same. SEO decides where you rank on a page of search results. AEO, answer engine optimisation, is about whether an AI assistant names your business at all, and what it says when it does.

    Which AI assistants do you check?

    The free audit asks four: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude. The dashboard tracks the first three every week.

    My industry is not on your list. Can you still help?

    Yes. Choose "Other" in the form. If your buyers ask an AI assistant before they choose, the audit works the same way. We write the questions for your business and check them before the audit runs.

    We sell online across India and have no city. Does this work for us?

    Yes. Type "India", or the market you sell in, in the city or market field. Your questions are then about your category, not about a city. On Managed, the Google profile line is replaced by the review or comparison site AI quotes most for you.

    Can you guarantee an AI will recommend us?

    No. Nobody controls those answers, and anyone who promises it is guessing. We change what the assistants read about you, and your dashboard shows every week whether the answers are changing.

    How soon will the answers change?

    We do not promise a date. You see the same numbers every week, so you know whether the work is having an effect, and you can stop whenever you choose.

    Do you need access to my website or accounts?

    Not for the audit; it uses only what is public. On Managed we need access to publish the page changes, and each one is published only after you approve it.

    Is the audit really free?

    Yes. No payment and no card. The report reaches your email within 24 hours and is yours even if you never speak to us again.
