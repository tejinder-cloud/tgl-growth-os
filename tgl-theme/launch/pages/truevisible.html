Questions

Questions about the dashboard.

What is TrueVisible?

It is The Growth Lab's dashboard for AI visibility. It asks ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity the questions your buyers ask, records who they name and which websites they quote, and gives you a ranked list of fixes.

When can I start using it?

It is in early access. Start with the free audit; dashboard invitations go out from December. You cannot sign up on your own yet.

Are these the same answers a person sees in the app?

Close to, not identical. The answers come from each assistant's own service with web search switched on. The same person can also get different answers on different days, which is why we ask every week and show you the count.

Who chooses the questions?

We propose them from your business, your buyers, your market and your languages, and a person checks each one before tracking starts. You can add or edit questions on the dashboard.

Do I need to install anything on my website?

No. Tracking uses only what is public. On Managed we need access to publish page changes, and each one goes live only after you approve it.

Why weekly and not daily?

What the assistants say about a business changes slowly. A weekly check is enough to see the direction, and it keeps the price where an owner-run business can afford it.

Can you guarantee an AI will recommend us?

No. Nobody controls those answers. The dashboard shows you every week whether the work is changing them.
