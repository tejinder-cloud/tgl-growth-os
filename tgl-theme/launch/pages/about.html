How we work

Four rules we hold ourselves to.

They apply to this website, to every report we send, and to every agent we build.

  1. RULE 1

    Show it, or do not say it.

    We make no claim we cannot show you on a screen today. Where we have no number, we leave a blank.

  2. RULE 2

    Count the work.

    Pages improved, listings fixed, answers read. You are told the number every month, not a feeling.

  3. RULE 3

    No promises about AI.

    Nobody controls what an AI assistant says. We change what it reads about you and show you whether the answers move.

  4. RULE 4

    A person signs off.

    Agents do the work. Anything that reaches a customer is approved by a person first.
