No, and nobody honestly can. We find out what the assistants say today, fix what they read about you, and show you every week whether it is changing.

A one-page report, by email within 24 hours: the questions we asked, which assistants named you, who they named instead, and the first fixes. No payment and no card.

Reply to enquiries, qualify leads, follow up on WhatsApp and email, prepare reports and handle routine back-office tasks. A person reviews their work.

With businesses anywhere in India. Everything we do runs online: the audit, the dashboard, the fixes and the monthly report.
