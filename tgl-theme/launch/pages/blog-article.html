What is AEO, and how is it different from SEO?

More buyers now ask an AI assistant who to choose. Here is what that changes for a business, in plain words.

THE SHORT ANSWER

SEO decides where your website appears on a page of search results. AEO, answer engine optimisation, is about whether an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity names your business when a buyer asks it for a recommendation, and whether what it says about you is right.

What AEO means

When someone searches on Google, they get a list of links and choose one. When they ask an AI assistant, they get an answer, often with only a few businesses named in it. If you are not one of those names, the buyer may never see you.

AEO is the work of finding out what the assistants say about your business today, and improving what they read about you so that the answer is more likely to include you, and to be correct.

How it differs from SEO

SEOAEO
The buyer seesA page of linksOne answer, with a few names
You are judged onYour position on the pageWhether you are named, and what is said
What gets readMostly your own websiteYour website, your listings, reviews and other sites that mention you
How you checkSearch for your keywordsAsk the assistants the questions your buyers ask, and record the answers

The two are related. A clear, well-organised website helps with both. But a business can rank well on Google and still be missing from AI answers, because the assistants also lean on sources outside your website.

Where the assistants read about you

An assistant builds its answer from what it can read. For a local business, that usually means three kinds of source:

  • Your own website. Clear pages about each service, with prices or ranges where you can give them, and answers to the questions buyers ask.
  • Your listings. Your Google Business Profile and the directories for your industry, with the same name, address, hours and services everywhere.
  • What others say. Reviews, guides and articles on other sites that mention you by name.

What you can do this month

  1. Write down the five questions a buyer would ask before choosing a business like yours.
  2. Ask each assistant those questions and save the answers word for word.
  3. Note who is named instead of you, and which websites the assistant quotes.
  4. Fix the easiest gap first. Often that is an incomplete profile on the directory the assistants quote most.
Key points
  • AEO is about being named in an AI answer, not about your rank on a page.
  • Assistants read your website, your listings and what others say about you.
  • Nobody can guarantee an assistant will name you. You can improve what it reads, and check every week whether the answers change.

Common questions

Do I still need SEO?

Yes. A clear website helps both search engines and AI assistants. AEO adds the work outside your website and the habit of checking the answers themselves.

Can anyone guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No. Nobody controls those answers. What you can do is improve what the assistants read about you and measure whether the answers change.

How often do AI answers change?

They can differ from day to day and from one person to the next. That is why it is worth asking the same questions on a schedule and counting, not judging from one answer.
