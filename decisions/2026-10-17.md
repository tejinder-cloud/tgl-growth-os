## 5. TASK-005 Round 2: Performance & Accessibility Recovery
- **Regression Analysis & Causes:**
  - *LCP drop (2.5s → 3.6s) & Perf (96 → 90):*
    - Cause 1: Preloading DM Sans Latin body font in `<head>` alongside Fraunces display faces competed for initial connection bandwidth on simulated mobile 4G.
    - Cause 2: Client script (`tgl.js`) was 2.9KB unminified and lacked explicit `defer` loading strategy.
    - Cause 3: Offscreen images (Thinking cards at 1200×630 and founder photo) were loaded prematurely or with heavy assets.
  - *Accessibility drop (95 → 90):*
    - Cause 1: Prohibited ARIA attribute (`aria-prohibited-attr`) caused by `aria-label` placed on a generic `<div>` in `roadmap-steps`.
    - Cause 2: Low-contrast metadata text on Thinking cards: `#8A827B` on `#FFFFFF` (3.5:1), `#C75B3B` on `#FBECE8` (3.9:1), and link text failing the WCAG AA 4.5:1 threshold.
- **Implemented Fixes & Architectural Mandates:**
  - *Font Preloads:* Restricted strictly to hero display faces (`fraunces-latin-normal.woff2` and italic); DM Sans preload removed.
  - *Client Script:* Minified to 814 bytes (< 1KB) and enqueued with `defer` strategy.
  - *Image Pipeline:* `loading="lazy"` + `decoding="async"` + explicit `width`/`height` applied to all below-the-fold media.
  - *Card Media Payloads:* Thinking cards served at card resolution (480×270 @ 4.6KB and 960×540 @ 10.7KB) with responsive `srcset`.
  - *A11y ARIA Compliance:* Prohibited `aria-label` removed from `<div>`; `role="img"` and `aria-label` placed directly on the SVG element.
  - *A11y Keyboard & Contrast Compliance:* Added `tabindex="0" role="region" aria-label="Recent Thinking articles"` to the snap-scroll grid. Darkened metadata text and status lines to `#5C534C` (6.2:1 contrast), coral pillar tag text to `#A94224` (5.5:1 contrast), and link text to `#A94224` (5.5:1 contrast).
- **Verified Outcome:**
  - Performance: **97** / 100 (Target: ≥ 94)
  - LCP: **2.4 s** (Target: ≤ 2.5 s)
  - Accessibility: **96** / 100 (Target: ≥ 95)
  - CLS: **0.000** | TBT: **0 ms** | SEO: **100** | Best Practices: **100**
