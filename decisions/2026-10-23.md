# Architectural & Product Decisions — 2026-10-23
**TrueVisible Product Decisions (from `products/truevisible/SPEC.md`)**

## 1. Plans and Pricing (SPEC.md §2)
- **Decision:** Establish a transparent multi-tier pricing structure—14-day free trial (no card), TrueVisible software at ₹3,999/mo ($49/mo USD), Managed AEO retainer upsell at ₹9,999/mo, and Agency at ₹4,500/mo (3 seats + ₹999/seat, ₹6,999/brand wholesale managed AEO)—with +18% GST in India and 0% GST under LUT for USD export sales.

## 2. Alerts and Communication Voice (SPEC.md §6)
- **Decision:** WhatsApp is restricted strictly to high-priority operational alerts (visibility drops, hallucinations, competitor overtakes, report links) delivered in a personal, plain-speaking tone signed "your TrueVisible team", with zero promotional broadcasts or automated jargon.

## 3. Execution Paths (SPEC.md §7)
- **Decision:** Delivery execution branches automatically by site platform at onboarding (WordPress via Easy MCP AI bridge; Shopify/Webflow/Wix/Squarespace via platform APIs; custom/none via a 30-minute developer change pack) while off-site optimisation and retainer pricing remain uniform across all platforms.

## 4. Agency White-Label & Billing Model (SPEC.md §9)
- **Decision:** The agency tier operates as a strict wholesale B2B2B white-label arrangement where TGL issues one consolidated monthly invoice exclusively to the agency partner, the agency sets retail pricing and bills its own brands, and TGL never contacts, invoices, or discloses its identity to white-label clients.
