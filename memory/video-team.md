# Memory — video-team
**Department:** marketing
**Created:** 2026-10-23

## Accumulated Heuristics & Rules
- Standardise all vector assets to `viewBox="0 0 120 120"` from `design-system/objects/`.
- Film spine: 6 scenes, ~15–20 seconds each, total runtime < 120s.
- Explanatory motion (cut-paper slip moving between trays, stamp pressing down, attribution loop back to Meta) is permitted; decorative float is banned.

## Pitfalls & Anti-Patterns Avoided
- No stock footage or AI-generated people.
- No corporate template music; sound bed must stay restrained and acoustic.
